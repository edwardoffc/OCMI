## In-place update — 2026-10-21

Reassess the assignment after two failed cycles or two preparation/review checkpoints without verified progress. One justified changed attempt may follow; another failure triggers one bounded process review before equivalent returns. Preserve STOP, safety gates and cross-task history. Worker development tests may evolve within AllowedPaths; independent acceptance remains protected. Validate new executable fixtures/adapters against actual calls/formats and a relevant control. No new role, tracker or automatic monitoring.

## What changes

- [Bounded waiting](../waiting.md) distinguishes execution, a named external event and no observed
  progress. A checkpoint retains the same operation/cursor, evidence, remaining action and next trigger.
  Prefer completion events; absent local policy, end observation after three unchanged polls per turn.
  This does not stop the operation or grant restart, monitoring, notifications or new authority.
- [Everyday personal memory](../personal-memory.md) covers undecided topics, another chat/AI system,
  explicit move/merge, stale facts, archive and selected-topic transfer/cloud conflicts. Each topic
  includes its on-demand GUIDE; a store registry is navigation, never a grant to read all topics.
- Risky nonstandard test premises need relevant control evidence before START. An unexplained
  setup/barrier failure needs discriminating diagnosis before speculative algorithm edits/retries.
  Known bounded preparation stays together; research needs a question, decision-changing evidence
  and stop condition. Reconcile affected current state while preserving fixed history.

No new role, mandatory report, runtime service or task-schema field. Work roles retain one acceptance
owner and protected checks. Ordinary personal conversations need no work protocol or transcript log.

protected packages, product state and WIP. Do not copy template BRIEF/PLAN/HANDOFF over live memory.
No automatic personal-store migration or real cloud upload follows this release.

## Verification and limits

check-continuity joins check-all on Windows/macOS; it verifies bilingual safeguards and synthetic
topic copying, identity, UTF-8, relative links, conflict preservation, archive and scope boundaries.
Other workflow, diagnostic, context, localization, source/ZIP and privacy gates remain required.
Publication requires local PS7/Windows PS5.1 and native Windows/macOS Apple Silicon/Intel CI,
with final results verified against the integrated commit and downloaded assets.

File checks do not certify live-agent obedience, token/time savings, external cloud isolation or
automatic cross-client recovery. They cannot repair or wake a frozen AI application/model turn.
Unsupported file access stays manual/incomplete; sharing and background work need explicit authority.
